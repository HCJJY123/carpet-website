## Selected implementation

### New URL

`/blog/commercial-carpet-floor-transition-planning-checklist`

- Buyer: importer, distributor, contractor, hotel/office/cinema project buyer, designer/specifier and installer coordinator.
- Stage: specification, quotation, sample/mock-up and pre-installation release.
- Primary keyword: `commercial carpet transition planning`.
- Related intent: commercial carpet floor transition checklist; carpet tile transition at doorway; carpet finished floor height; commercial flooring junction schedule.
- SEO title: `Commercial Carpet Floor Transition Planning Checklist | VCARPETS`.
- Meta: `Plan commercial carpet transitions by recording floor build-up, adjacent finishes, door clearance, wheeled traffic, edge details and approval responsibility.`
- H1: `Commercial Carpet Floor Transition Planning Checklist`.
- Direct answer: approve each junction using both complete floor assemblies, actual location/use, edge detail and named responsibility; carpet thickness or sample approval alone is insufficient.

### Buyer and conversion change

The article supplies a junction schedule, complete-build-up review, door and wheeled-route checks, responsibility matrix, mock-up boundary, copyable brief, FAQ and a source-prefilled contact CTA. The quote brief asks for application, area by zone, carpet format, plan, junction schedule/photos, adjacent finishes, build-ups, traffic, destination, documents, sample purpose and timing. Sample format/fee/timing, MOQ, availability, production timing and product performance remain construction/project confirmed.
