1. `/solutions/hotel-hospitality`
   - Replaces unsupported category-wide ASTM/EN, material, gauge, width, wear-class, acoustic, MOQ and delivery commitments.
   - Adds a direct supplier answer, five hotel zones, product routes, evidence boundaries, five-step approval sequence, FAQ and source-aware RFQ prefill.
   - Keeps custom pattern/colour availability and paid samples conditional on project confirmation.
2. `/solutions/office-carpet-tiles-supplier`
   - Replaces universal 50x50, 200–500 sqm MOQ, backing-performance and overseas-sample claims with named-product, packing, backing/fixing and written-quote checks.
   - Retains existing product routes and inquiry handlers.
3. `/blog/carpet-tiles-vs-broadloom-commercial-projects-guide`
   - Preserves its permanent URL and original publication date; modification date becomes 2026-11-07.
   - Adds a complete office/hotel shortlist, category-to-product quote paths and a qualification shortcut.
