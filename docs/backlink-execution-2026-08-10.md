# Vishomecarpet Backlink Execution Plan — 2026-08-10

## Scope

This plan supports the two new procurement articles:

- `/blog/hotel-carpet-renovation-hard-flooring-decision-guide`
- `/blog/office-carpet-tiles-rolling-chairs-fitout-guide`

The objective is to earn relevant editorial mentions and referral visits from hospitality design, commercial flooring, workplace fit-out, architecture, facility-management and B2B procurement sources. It does not use paid link schemes, automated directory spam, forum flooding or undisclosed sponsored placements.

## Target Prospect Types

Prioritize relevance and editorial fit over domain metrics:

1. Hospitality design publications and hotel renovation resources.
2. Commercial flooring, carpet and installation publications.
3. Workplace design, office fit-out and commercial real-estate resources.
4. Facility-management and building-operations communities.
5. Architecture and interior-design resource pages that accept practical guides.
6. Regional supplier, installer and contractor associations in the United States, Canada and Australia.
7. B2B procurement newsletters and construction-material resource pages.
8. Genuine professional discussions where a complete answer is useful without forcing a link.

Do not treat a site as a prospect merely because it offers a “guest post” package. Reject pages with unrelated outbound-link lists, obvious link exchanges, spun content, or unclear editorial ownership.

## Editorial Pitch Angles

### Hotel renovation angle

Offer a concise reference for teams deciding between carpet and hard flooring by zone. The useful contribution is the decision framework: corridor noise, luggage wheels, comfort, pattern scale, cleaning route, sample approval and repair stock.

### Office fit-out angle

Offer a practical checklist for rolling-chair zones and leased offices. The useful contribution is the link between pile, backing, caster movement, substrate, installation method, make-good obligations and phased replacement.

## Anchor Text Guardrails

Use natural anchors and rotate them by context:

- 35% branded: `Vishomecarpet`, `Vishome technical guide`.
- 25% naked URL or page title.
- 25% descriptive partial match: `hotel renovation carpet checklist`, `office carpet tile fit-out guide`.
- 15% product/category context: `wall-to-wall hotel carpet`, `commercial carpet tiles`.

These are operating targets, not a requirement to force an exact ratio. A nofollow, sponsored or unlinked mention can still be useful for referral traffic and brand discovery.

## 30-Day Execution Rhythm

### Days 1–3: Prepare and qualify

- Verify the two Blog URLs, canonical tags, HTTP 200, page title and visible author/date.
- Build 20–30 prospects across the target types.
- Remove sites with unrelated content or suspicious link patterns.
- Add a one-sentence personalization note for every retained prospect.

### Days 4–10: First outreach

- Send a small batch of 5–8 personalized messages per asset.
- Offer the guide as a reference or propose a short expert contribution.
- Use one relevant destination page per message; do not send a bundle of links.
- Record delivery, response and follow-up dates.

### Days 11–17: Follow-up and community contribution

- Send one concise follow-up only where appropriate.
- Answer relevant professional questions with a complete, non-promotional explanation.
- Link only when the full guide directly supports the answer and the community permits it.

### Days 18–24: Publish and expand

- Review accepted edits, contributor drafts and resource-page inclusions.
- Confirm that any published link is contextually relevant and points to the correct canonical URL.
- Request corrections only for broken or misleading destination links.

### Days 25–30: Measure and refine

- Compare referral sessions, engaged sessions, assisted inquiries and branded search impressions.
- Keep prospects that respond or send qualified traffic.
- Pause low-quality sources and refine the subject line or angle.
- Add a new resource only when it answers a real buyer question better than the current assets.

## Reddit and Community Rule

Reddit discussions are editorial input, not a backlink inventory. The recurring buyer concerns used in the two articles include hotel noise, stains, comfort, rolling-chair movement, chair mats, phased tile replacement, adhesive removal and leased-office make-good work. Do not copy comments, imply endorsement, mass-post links, or identify individual users. Participate only when the answer is complete without the link.

## Measurement

Track the following in GA4/GSC and the outreach sheet:

- Referring domain and landing page.
- New users and engaged sessions.
- Scroll or page-depth signal where available.
- `quote_form_start` and `form_submit` events.
- Email and WhatsApp click events.
- Assisted conversion path within 30 days.
- New referring domains that are relevant and indexable.

Do not judge a placement only by whether it passes link equity. A relevant referral visit from a project buyer can be more valuable than a high-metric page with no commercial audience.
