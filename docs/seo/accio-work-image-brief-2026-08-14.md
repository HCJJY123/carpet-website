# Accio Work Image Brief for Two Reddit-Informed Blog Drafts

Purpose: generate on-brand hero and support images for the two draft blog posts below. Keep the subject realistic, commercial, and procurement-focused. Do not add logos, watermarks, fake certifications, invented hotel names, or readable brand text in the image.

## Global image rules

- Style: realistic B2B commercial flooring photography
- Format: wide banner hero proportions, clean editorial lighting
- Mood: premium, practical, procurement-ready, not advertising-heavy
- Avoid: stock-photo cliché, exaggerated glow, cartoon styling, visible brand names, fake certificates, oversaturated colors, messy clutter
- Composition: leave negative space for headline overlays, keep floor product and use case clearly visible
- If text appears in the image, it must be minimal and non-dominant; ideally none

## 1) Hotel Corridor Carpet That Hides Stains Without Looking Dirty

### Hero image prompt

Create a realistic wide banner image of a modern hotel corridor with patterned broadloom carpet designed to hide normal soil and wear. Show a clean but lived-in hospitality corridor with warm wall lighting, subtle luggage wheel traffic, discreet housekeeping presence in the distance, and a patterned carpet in layered charcoal, taupe and muted gold tones. The carpet should look premium, quiet, and stain-conscious, not busy or chaotic. Leave generous empty space on one side for headline text. Ultra-detailed, natural perspective, commercial hospitality photography, no logos, no watermark, no readable signage.

### Support image prompt 1

Create a close-up commercial flooring image showing hotel corridor carpet pattern scale, fiber texture, and seam detail under warm corridor lighting. Emphasize how the tonal pattern hides footprints and small soil marks while still looking refined. Natural macro perspective, realistic texture, no branding, no text.

### Support image prompt 2

Create a hotel maintenance planning scene showing a housekeeping trolley near a corridor carpet section, with clean geometry, controlled lighting, and a visible difference between traffic zone and quieter zone. Focus on the carpet’s maintenance-friendly visual behavior rather than cleaning action. Realistic hospitality interior, no logos, no clutter, no text.

### Negative prompt

Avoid cartoon carpet, high-contrast neon patterns, empty corridor stock photo look, obvious dirt piles, water stains, fake hotel branding, visible people faces, exaggerated luxury shine, messy service equipment.

## 2) Office Carpet Tiles Renovation Cycle Procurement Guide

### Hero image prompt

Create a realistic wide banner image of a corporate office renovation using modular carpet tiles. Show phased installation in a modern office with desks, rolling chairs, partial furniture movement, and clearly defined work zones. The carpet tiles should look commercial, low-pile, stable, and practical for renovation cycles. Use neutral tones with a subtle geometric pattern and a professional fit-out atmosphere. Leave space for overlay text. Natural daylight and soft artificial light, editorial commercial photography, no logos, no watermark, no readable signage.

### Support image prompt 1

Create a close-up image of office carpet tiles with visible modular seams, low-pile surface, and rolling chair path context. Show the surface as stable, repairable, and suitable for commercial office use. Keep the image realistic and technical, with precise material texture and no brand marks.

### Support image prompt 2

Create a phased office fit-out scene with one half of the workspace finished in carpet tiles and the other half under controlled renovation. Include storage boxes, moved chairs, and a clean install process without construction mess. The image should communicate speed, replacement flexibility, and renovation practicality. No logos, no marketing props, no readable text.

### Negative prompt

Avoid plush residential carpet, huge decorative tiles, neon office colors, construction debris everywhere, empty showroom look, visible company logos, fake awards, blurry chair wheels, over-staged stock photography.

## Delivery notes for Accio Work

- Deliver one hero image and two support images for each blog.
- Preserve a wide composition so the blog layout can overlay title text.
- Keep the carpet product visually dominant, but the use case must be obvious.
- If needed, create 1600px and 900px crops for responsive web use.
