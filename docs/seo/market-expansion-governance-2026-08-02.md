# Country Market SEO Governance - 2026-08-02

## Decision

VISHOME should pause broad country-page expansion after the current market set and focus on improving evidence, conversions, internal links, GSC coverage and lead quality for priority markets.

## Priority Markets

The current priority markets for deeper content review are:

- Russia
- Australia
- Philippines
- Poland
- Mexico

Peru and Colombia remain priority pages for gold mining carpet mat demand rather than general commercial carpet sourcing.

## Expansion Rules

- Add no new country landing page unless there is clear search demand, inquiry history, distributor demand or paid-search validation.
- Do not publish a local certification, stock claim, lead time promise, warranty claim or delivery route unless it is supported by project evidence or a written supplier record.
- Keep country pages focused on buying decisions: product fit, climate, site conditions, documentation, packing, freight and RFQ information.
- Review GSC index coverage, impressions, clicks and lead quality before adding more countries.
- Prefer improving existing pages with local buyer questions, FAQ, commercial terms and product links instead of creating thin new pages.

## Next Review

Run the next country-page review after GSC has enough data for the August 2026 additions. The review should compare indexed URLs, impressions, clicks, quote-form starts, email clicks and qualified lead records by market.
