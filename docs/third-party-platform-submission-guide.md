# Third-Party Platform Submission Guide

Last updated: 2026-08-06

## Important Boundary

Codex does not register accounts, bypass verification, pay platform fees, submit forms automatically or send outreach emails. Human review is required for all third-party submissions.

## Recommended Submission Order

1. Product/specification platforms after company and product data are confirmed.
2. Project partners after project names, images and attribution text are approved.
3. Hospitality and office design media with practical technical topics.
4. Flooring trade media and installer resources.
5. Associations, trade shows and verified business directories.

## Do Not Submit Until Confirmed

- Certifications or test reports.
- Named client or hotel projects.
- Exact factory figures not already approved for public use.
- Revit, Archicad, SketchUp or IFC files that have not been professionally prepared.
- Any contact email or sender name that is not approved by the business owner.
