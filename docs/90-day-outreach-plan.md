# 90-Day Outreach Plan

Last updated: 2026-08-06

## Days 1-30: Asset Readiness and First Contacts

- Finish PDF landing page review and document version confirmation.
- Confirm company facts, sender name and approved public contact details.
- Prioritize 8 project partner prospects and 5 product/specification platform prospects.
- Fill real domains, submission URLs and contact forms in `data/link-prospects.csv`.
- Send no more than one first-touch email per verified prospect.
- Track every action in `data/outreach-log.csv`.

Target outcome: 20 verified prospects contacted and 3-5 relevant referring domains secured or under review.

## Days 31-60: Platform Packages and Editorial Outreach

- Prepare platform-specific product submission packs for office carpet tiles, hospitality broadloom and custom Axminster planning.
- Confirm certificates and test documents that can be shared publicly.
- Pitch practical editorial topics to hospitality, office design and flooring media.
- Follow up after 7 days, then 10 days later if no response.

Target outcome: 50 total verified prospects, 8-15 relevant referring domains secured or under review.

## Days 61-90: Partner References and BIM/CAD Preparation

- Request project partner references only after project permissions are approved.
- Prepare verified BIM/CAD metadata and decide whether a professional BIM service is needed.
- Review traffic and inquiry quality from referral sources.
- Keep only sources that produce relevant visitors, document downloads or quote-form starts.

Target outcome: 90 total verified prospects, 15-25 relevant referring domains over the full cycle.

## Rejection Rules

- Reject PBNs, bulk directory packages, irrelevant guest posts, forum-signature links, comment spam and paid links without editorial relevance.
- Reject any platform that requires unsupported claims, fake reviews, fake client logos or hidden links.
