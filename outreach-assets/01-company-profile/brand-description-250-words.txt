Vishome Carpet supplies commercial carpet tiles, hospitality broadloom, public-area carpets and custom carpet solutions for international B2B projects. The company works with project buyers that need a practical route from product selection to sample review, quotation, production planning, packing and delivery coordination.

The product scope includes office carpet tiles, hotel corridor and guestroom carpets, custom printed carpet, public-area carpet, custom sculpted lobby rugs, natural sisal carpet and specialist products such as gold mining carpet mats. Buyers can use the website to review product categories, case references, technical library downloads and contact forms for project quotation requests.

For third-party product platforms, project partners and trade-media references, Vishome Carpet should only publish confirmed facts. Product performance, fire documents, acoustic data, antistatic data, MOQ, lead time, packing and price must be matched to the exact construction and written quotation. Named hotels, designers, contractors, distributors, project area and project photography should be used only after approval.
