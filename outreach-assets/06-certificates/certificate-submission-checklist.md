# Certificate Submission Checklist

Do not submit certificate claims until current documents are confirmed.

Required manual checks:

- CONFIRM_CERTIFICATIONS
- CONFIRM_TEST_REPORTS
- CONFIRM_PRODUCT_CONSTRUCTION_MATCH
- CONFIRM_DOCUMENT_EXPIRY_OR_VALIDITY
- CONFIRM_PUBLIC_SHARING_PERMISSION

Recommended public URL until confirmed:

https://www.vishomecarpet.com/certifications

Use only construction-specific and current documents for third-party platforms.
